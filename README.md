## 🌟 KEY ENGINEERING INNOVATIONS

### 1. 🎨 Video-Capture Ready High-Contrast Light Theme
- Engineered specifically for recording high-definition video walkthroughs and mobile camera captures without screen wash-out or sensor flare.
- Crisp architectural off-white foundation (`#F4F5F7`), slate structural card borders (`#CBD5E1`), and rich dark navy typography (`#0F172A`) for maximum legibility.
- Inner CAD viewport features a light-gray blueprint grid (`0x0284c7` / `#cbd5e1`) and calibrated ambient fill lighting (`0.95` intensity) making metallic Inconel, Chrome, SX500, and GRCop-42 copper pop vividly.

### 2. 🖐️ 21-Node Real-Time AI Computer Vision Matrix
- Powered by **Google MediaPipe Hands AI** running directly on client-side WebGL / CPU pipelines at a locked **60 FPS**.
- Classifies hand gestures using 3D Euclidean vector kinematics across fingertip, PIP, MCP, and wrist coordinates.
- Skeletons are rendered in high-luminance neon green (`#00FF66` / `#10B981`) with dark outer drop-shadow borders, ensuring razor-sharp contrast against any background or lighting condition.
- **Dual-Hand Interlock Engine**: Prevents accidental false positives by requiring simultaneous dual-fist confirmation for catastrophic engine disassembly.

### 3. 💥 10,888 Procedural Parts Exploded-View Kinematics
- Full hierarchical procedural breakdown of the SpaceX Raptor 3 engine:
  - **Combustion Chamber & Bell Nozzle Shell**: Inconel 718 with aerodynamic expansion curve.
  - **GRCop-42 Regenerative Jacket**: 3,600 micro-channels 3D-printed directly into the chamber liner.
  - **Coaxial Injector Elements**: 458 swirl injector assemblies arranged in 6 concentric rings.
  - **Dual Turbopump Rotors & Volutes**: 968 SX500 high-temperature alloy blading segments.
  - **Structural Fastener Array**: 1,648 A286 aerospace double-hex studs and bolt flanges.
  - **Internal Manifold Lattices**: 1,500 internal regenerative cooling fins.
  - **TVC Actuators & Gimbal Mount**: Dual electromechanical gimbal actuators.
- Parts expand smoothly outward along calculated radial and axial separation vectors:
  $$\mathbf{p}(t) = \mathbf{p}_{\text{base}} + \vec{v}_{\text{explode}} \cdot f_{\text{explode}} + \mathbf{o}_{\text{isolated}}$$

### 4. 🔥 Supersonic Exhaust Plume & Thermal CFD Simulation
- **1,400-Particle Supersonic Jet Plume**: High-velocity vertex-colored particle stream responding directly to throttle intensity.
- **4 Dynamic Mach Diamond Shock Cones**: Pulsing diamond shock waves with additive blending simulating atmospheric supersonic expansion.
- **Thermal CFD Emissive Heatmap**: Internal chamber and nozzle glow transitions from cool copper to bright incandescent orange-red (`0xff3300`) as throttle ramps from 0% to 100%.

### 5. 🔊 Procedural Web Audio Engine
- Synthesizes 100% procedural audio in real time with zero external audio assets:
  - Low-frequency combustion roar via a white noise buffer filtered through a dynamic `BiquadFilterNode` lowpass filter ($110\text{ Hz}$).
  - Exponential spark decay sound simulating pre-burner torch igniter pulses.
